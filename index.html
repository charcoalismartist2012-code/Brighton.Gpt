About Us

Shaping Future Leaders Since 2010

Brighton International School is a co-educational CBSE-affiliated institution committed to holistic education and innovation.

8-Acre Lush Green Campus

A Chronicle of Excellence

Established in 2010, Brighton International School has consistently been ranked as the No. 1 Co-Ed School in Chhattisgarh by Education World Magazine. Our 8-acre campus in Nardaha, near the Vidhan Sabha, provides a conducive environment for learning and growth.

We follow a progressive, inquiry-based curriculum aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP), integrating academics with socio-emotional well-being and life skills through our unique "Brighton Way" for lesson planning.
